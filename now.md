# Now

What I'm actively working on, warts and all. Updated whenever, not dated like a post.

## Bowling ball physics

Deriving and simulating equations of motion for a bowling ball's full trajectory — sliding, rolling, and the transition between — using SymPy's `physics.mechanics` module (Kane's Method) and quaternion-based attitude integration. Working through Hopkins & Patterson's paper as a reference. Currently in Jupyter notebooks; not yet its own repo.
