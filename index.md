# Welcome to Bryan's Place

## Repurposing old smartphones

Welcome to my corner of the web. I like to breathe new life into "obsolete" phones — specifically turning them into functional, low-power homelabs.

After looking at loads of alternatives, I homed in on **PostmarketOS**. The big plus for me is its ability to run Docker, and it installs without rooting the phone via its installer, Pmbootstrap. I have two Xiaomi A1 devices that have been running for over three years with no problems.

The aim of this site is to supplement the excellent [PostmarketOS documentation](https://wiki.postmarketos.org) by focusing on my real-world experience with one model.
